The Election Commission Row & Delhi Protests

Massive civil unrest has hit the heart of New Delhi, with thousands of protestors marching towards the Election Commission of India (ECI) headquarters. A broad coalition comprising the Samyukt Kisan Morcha (SKM), student unions, and prominent opposition leaders are demanding the immediate resignation and prosecution of Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar. The outcry follows an investigative exposure revealing that the CEC bypassed internal written objections from other commissioners to push through a Special Intensive Revision (SIR) of the country's electoral rolls. The revision resulted in the sudden, mass deletion of an estimated 4.2 million voters in Delhi alone, with many living citizens erroneously registered as "dead" or dropped due to vague "logical discrepancies". While the ECI asserts the cleanup was meant to purify voter databases, the issue has sparked intense political deadlock ahead of key upcoming state assembly elections.
