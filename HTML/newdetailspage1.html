The 7.0-Magnitude Tibet Earthquake

A powerful 7.0-magnitude earthquake struck a high-altitude, remote region of northern Tibet, leaving at least 16 people dead and dozens injured. The seismic event, which triggered a high-level emergency response from the State Council Earthquake Relief Headquarters, had its epicentre in Maru township within Baqên County, Nagqu. Occurring at a shallow depth, the violent tremors shook structures up to 400 kilometres away in the capital city of Lhasa and caused over 339 houses to entirely collapse. Landslides and rockfalls have blocked crucial paths like National Highway 317, severely hampering the early flow of emergency vehicles. More than 12,400 residents have been evacuated to emergency tents and prefabricated shelters as search and rescue teams continue digging through debris.
