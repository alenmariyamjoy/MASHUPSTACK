India-China Border Standoff in Arunachal Pradesh

In the first official public confirmation of the scale of recent frontier border tensions, Indo-Tibetan Border Police (ITBP) Director General Shatrujeet Kapur confirmed that Indian and Chinese troops remain locked in active face-offs at multiple locations along the Line of Actual Control (LAC). Tensions initially flared during routine patrolling near the remote Maja operating base in the Taksing circle of Upper Subansiri district, Arunachal Pradesh. According to security sources, China’s People's Liberation Army (PLA) later returned in larger numbers to pitch military tents at an elevated location roughly 6 to 7 kilometres inside India’s perception of the LAC. The military standoff has persisted for weeks, prompting India to rapidly scale up its local troop deployments, deploy surveillance drones, and reinforce forward positions.
