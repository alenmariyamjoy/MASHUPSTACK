Saudi Arabia Airport & Infrastructure Attacks

Tensions in the Middle East have severely impacted the Indian diaspora after Yemen's Houthi rebels executed a series of drone and missile strikes targeting critical civilian and economic infrastructure inside Saudi Arabia. An attack hitting King Fahd International Airport in the eastern city of Dammam tragically killed an Indian resident and a Saudi national, while wounding eight others. Concurrently, air defences intercepted a drone aiming for a Saudi Aramco facility at the Shaybah oilfield, where another Indian worker was caught in the impact and injured. The escalating regional conflict has forced major airlines like IndiGo to issue sudden flight cancellations, leaving thousands of travellers and pilgrims stranded.
