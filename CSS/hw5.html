Five Habits of a Productive Developer

By Meera Nair

Good developers are not always the fastest typists.
They plan before they code, read error messages carefully, and take short breaks to stay focused. Over time, these small habits save hours of work and make projects easier to maintain.

Clean code is written for humans first and computers second.
